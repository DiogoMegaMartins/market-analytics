# Métricas sectoriais

## Banking
NIM = Net Interest Income / average interest-earning assets; CET1 = CET1 capital / RWA; NPL ratio = NPL / gross loans; Cost-to-Income = operating expenses / operating income. Preservar scope (solo/consolidated), transitional/fully-loaded e definições regulatórias.

## Insurance
P&C: Loss Ratio, Expense Ratio, Combined Ratio, Reserve Development, Solvency, Premium Growth e Reinsurance Dependence. Life e Reinsurance exigem modelos próprios. Preservar IFRS 17, IFRS 4, US GAAP, statutory e Solvency II; não misturar bases.

## Equity REIT
FFO, AFFO, per-share, same-property NOI growth, occupancy, Net Debt/EBITDAre, interest coverage e AFFO payout. mREIT separado. AFFO é frequentemente definido pela empresa; distinguir reportado de estimativa interna.

## Software & Technology
SaaS: ARR/MRR, NRR, GRR, CAC, LTV, LTV/CAC e Rule of 40. RPO não equivale a ARR. Hardware: gross margin, inventory turns, warranty costs, backlog, book-to-bill e R&D intensity. Definições empresariais não são automaticamente comparáveis.
